# Annual Leave Policy

Document code: HR-LEAVE-001

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least five working days before the requested start date. The employee's manager approves or declines the request in the portal. Unused leave carryover is limited to five days and must be used by March 31 of the following year. Emergency absence should be reported to the manager as soon as reasonably possible and recorded in the HR portal on return.

Employees can review their current leave balance and benefits in the HR portal. Questions about eligibility or corrections should be directed to HR.
